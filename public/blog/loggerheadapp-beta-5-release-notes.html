Loggerhead.app Beta 5: Release Notes

Published on May 27, 2025 | 2 min read

🎉 Loggerhead Beta 5: AI, Precision, and Total Control

Loggerhead Beta 5 isn’t just a feature update — it’s a full leap forward in control, insight, and performance. This release introduces powerful stat tools, smarter replay systems, and exclusive features for subscribers that give you a competitive edge. This release also introduces features exclusive to our subscribers (including Active Beta Users).

💡 AI Match Recaps (Subscriber Only)

You log the match — we write the story.

Beta 5 introduces AI-generated match summaries, with selectable tone:

  • Factual – for records and reporting

  • Playful – for fun and social sharing

  • Coach’s Report – for tactical feedback

These recaps appear automatically after each match and are saved with your data.

📊 Team Stats Tracking (Subscriber Only)

Team-level stats are here.

Subscribers can now:

  • Track totals across all matches

  • See team scoring trends

This brings full-program visibility into team performance.

🎯 Advanced Stat Logging — Now Optional

There’s a new Advanced Logging toggle the Volleyball Court.


When enabled, it unlocks precision tools for serious tracking:

  • Serve Zone Overlays
    Tap to log where the serve was aimed or landed

  • Serve Error Types
    Record whether a miss was in the net, out, or a foot fault

  • Opponent Ace Targeting
    When the opponent lands an ace, you now tag which of your players was targeted

Don’t need all that? Turn Advanced Logging off and get a streamlined stat experience.

↩️ All-New Undo Logic

Undo is now rock solid.

  • Rewinds court position, ball side, and serve state

  • Strikes through invalid log entries

  • Adjusts score and touches cleanly

Whether you're correcting a tap or reversing a full play chain, it just works.

🎥 Referee Replay Logic — Smarter Than Ever

Beta 5 refines how Loggerhead reacts to reversals.

When a point is overturned (out call, whistle error, foot fault), the system:

  • Rolls back the right stats

  • Assigns the point correctly

  • Keeps your court and server flow intact

No manual edits needed. Ref calls are now respected.
